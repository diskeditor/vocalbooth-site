How it works

  1. 1

    The sender opens Connect Studio, is walked through an easy setup for their specific project and invites users.

  2. 2

    The talent or clients receive an email with a link to install the Connect Talent app if they don't already have it, as well as a connection link. The app walks them through an easy setup.

  3. 3

    Talent can now see and hear the program, as well as the other talent in the room, and have their audio recorded to a DAW as individual tracks. Or clients can hear a mix, discuss and give notes.
