Connect, Record, Review

No more juggling more than one app or complicated hardware. Connect puts studio-quality sound, picture, talkback and video conferencing into one place, built for post-production.

A pair of Mac apps for remote voice-over, ADR, loop group and client review sessions. The sender sends the program picture and sound. The talent can send back clean, full-quality audio, each person on their own channel ready to record. Or clients can watch and listen and give review notes, even drawing on the picture for added clarity.

How it works

  1. 1

    The sender opens Connect Studio and clicks Invite users.

  2. 2

    Everyone else clicks the link, installs Connect Talent (free) and joins.

  3. 3

    Talent hear the program and the talkback and see the picture. The sender records every voice on its own track.

Connect Studio

For the sender. Licensed.

Every voice on its own track

Choose Individual outputs and each person comes out on their own channel, straight into their own Pro Tools track. Choose Stereo output and everyone is mixed on 1–2 for reviews or simple listening.

Send the program

Choose a program audio input and the talent hear it: Conduit Send from Pro Tools, any Core Audio device, or a Network Video Sender on your network. A LISTEN button lets you check what you're sending before anyone joins.

Send the picture

Picture can come from your screen, a Blackmagic capture card, NDI or a Network Video Sender. Choose the quality (2 to 12 Mbps) to suit everyone's connection. Optionally, put each person's name over the picture as a watermark.

Talkback that stays out of the way

A separate talkback mic goes to the talent on its own stream, so they can set program and talkback at their own levels. Talk is momentary or latching, and the space bar works it.

See everyone

The Talent column shows each person's camera, name, level meter and connection bars. You can mute them or set their level, and you can open everyone full-size in their own window.

Draw on the picture

Mark up the picture to point at a sync problem or a line. Each person's marks are in their own colour with their name, and they dissolve away on their own. Pin a mark to keep it, and choose whether talent may draw too.

Invite in seconds

Every room has a code and a password. Invite users emails a link that installs Connect Talent and drops them into your room. You can save groups of addresses and move them between Macs. Send from your Mail app or from web mail such as Gmail or Outlook.

Set up in a minute

The first time it opens, Connect Studio walks you through it:

  1. your name
  2. stereo or individual outputs
  3. program audio input
  4. audio output
  5. picture input
  6. camera

Returning? Skip setup and you're straight in.

Connect Talent

For the talent, the client, anyone you invite. Free.

Made for people who just want to show up

Click the link in the invitation and Connect Talent installs. Friendly cards then walk you through it:

  1. your name
  2. the kind of session (voice-over / ADR / loop group, or client review)
  3. your microphone, with a live meter
  4. your headphones
  5. your camera, or a picture of yourself if you'd rather

Then press the glowing Join Room button.

Hear the studio your way

The program and the studio's talkback have their own level and mute. In a client room, you can mute the program and just hear the talent.

Sound your best

Your mic is sent at full quality. Optional noise reduction (Light or Strong) helps in a less-than-perfect room.

See the session

Watch the studio's picture and everyone's cameras in a gallery that resizes as people join. Go Big Picture with one click, and draw on the picture when the studio allows it.

Talk back

For voice-over and ADR, your talk button starts latched on. For client reviews it's hold-to-talk. Either way the space bar works it.

Under the hood

Audio
Opus, with jitter buffering and clock drift correction; latency settings for nearby or long-distance talent.
Picture
H.264 hardware encoding, adjustable from 2 to 12 Mbps.
Privacy
Sessions are encrypted with a key made from the room's code and password. Our server only passes the data along and can't listen in.
Mac
Both apps run on macOS 12.3 or later.
Works with
Pro Tools (through Conduit) · Blackmagic capture cards · NDI · Parabolic's Network Video Sender
